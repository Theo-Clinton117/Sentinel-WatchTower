Emergency Disclaimer

Sentinel-Watchtower is an informational and coordination support platform. It is not an emergency dispatch provider.

In any immediate threat, injury, fire, crime in progress, or urgent medical event, contact local emergency services directly.

Alert timing, map data, and community reports may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable due to connectivity, operational, or third-party limitations.

Use all safety information with independent judgment and follow instructions from official authorities first.
