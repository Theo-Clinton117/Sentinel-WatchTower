About Sentinel-Watchtower

We are building safety technology that keeps communities informed, connected, and prepared.

Our Story

Sentinel-Watchtower started from a simple goal: give people timely, reliable safety information so they can make better decisions in everyday life and emergencies. We saw a gap between official alerts and what residents actually receive in real time, and we built this platform to close that gap.

Our Process

1. Collect

Receive reports and data signals from trusted channels and community inputs.

2. Verify

Cross-check details before broadcasting to reduce noise and increase trust.

3. Respond

Deliver actionable alerts quickly so people can move to safer options.

Our Team

Our team combines public safety focus, product design, and engineering to deliver a dependable platform for communities.
